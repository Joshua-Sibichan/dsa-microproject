## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the hospitality, luxury, and international sophistication of world-class long-haul aviation. It targets global travellers, high-net-worth individuals, premium business flyers, and discerning vacationers who demand effortless, high-trust digital interactions alongside an aspirational aesthetic. 

The emotional response evoked must be one of calm assurance, bespoke luxury, and anticipation of an elevated journey. The visual style merges **Minimalist Modern Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- High-contrast, clean layout grids layered over luminous warm-ivory and alabaster surfaces.
- Selective champagne-gold detailing that signals distinction without visual clutter.
- Rich crimson focal points engineered for purposeful navigational velocity and conversion.
- Architectural, crisp structural alignments reminiscent of private aircraft cabin craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette balances heritage grandeur with functional clarity:
- **Primary (`#D71921`)**: The definitive crimson brand mark. Used strictly for high-impact calls to action, active booking steps, and critical selection cues. Avoid overusing across massive backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted champagne gold representing First Class privileges, luxury upgrades, and concierge indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#1B2232`)**: Deep nocturnal navy, grounding the system with crisp contrast for typography, flight routes, and aircraft silhouettes.
- **Neutral (`#F9F8F6`)**: Warm porcelain and alabaster background base that prevents digital fatigue and replaces sterile, harsh whites.

### Cabin Tier Semantics
The aircraft seat selection map demands unmistakable visual clarity:
- **First Class Private Suite**: `suite-first` (`#9E7B35`) with soft `gold-light` accents.
- **Business Class Lie-Flat**: `seat-business` (`#1B2232`) with sleek hairline outlines.
- **Premium Economy**: `seat-prem-econ` (`#2B5273`) bridging executive comfort with broad accessibility.
- **Economy & Preferred Legroom**: `seat-economy` (`#4A5568`), with available states highlighted by subtle crisp borders, and occupied states dropped to non-interactive `seat-occupied` (`#D2D6DC`).

## Typography

The type scale combines the editorial majesty of **Noto Serif** with the pristine legibility and engineering balance of **Work Sans**:
- **Display & Headlines (`Noto Serif`)**: Communicates bespoke prestige, editorial warmth, and heritage. Applied to cabin tier introductions, destination headers, flight overview milestones, and confirmation summaries.
- **Body & Operational Labels (`Work Sans`)**: Optimized for high-density booking contexts, flight times, IATA codes (e.g., DXB → LHR), aircraft specification badges (A380-800, B777-300ER), seat matrices, and price breakdowns.
- **Monetary & Time Displays**: Price figures and departure/arrival timings use medium or semi-bold weights in `Work Sans` with tabular numeric alignment to guarantee vertical scanability in search matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system governs desktop interfaces, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Booking Flow Architecture
- **Desktop Flight Search & Results**: Employs an asymmetric split: an 8-column primary stream displaying expandable flight result cards and cabin upgrades, paired with a 4-column sticky sidebar presenting the live Fare Summary, taxes, and baggage allowances.
- **Seat Map Explorer (A380 / Boeing 777)**: Configured as a dual-pane canvas. The left or central panel holds the vertical scrollable fuselage cross-section; the right pane displays the interactive seat inspector (recline degree, pitch in inches, 3D preview, onboard lounge proximity).
- **Spatial Rhythm**: Generous vertical section margins (`space-xl`) between flight segments cultivate an uncluttered, premium reading cadence, while dense micro-components (layover indicators, seat amenity badges) adhere to compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm shadows** and **tonal layering**, strictly avoiding cold, dark drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Fuselage & Background Canvas)**: Solid `#F9F8F6` canvas with subtle hairline dividing rules (`#E5E1D8`) defining structural boundaries.
- **Level 1 (Card & Flight Result Surfaces)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` planes supported by an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 34, 50, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Selected Tiers, Flyouts)**: Lifted planes with soft dual-layer shadow: `0 10px 28px -4px rgba(27, 34, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(27, 34, 50, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Summary & Modals)**: Floated surface with deep ambient depth: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 20, 30, 0.12)`.
- **Gold Accent Elevation**: First Class and special fare tier highlights leverage an inset edge gleam via a 1px border colored with `#C5A059` at 40% opacity rather than loud glow effects.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** shape language. Sharp corners feel overly clinical, whereas pill or highly rounded shapes undermine the architectural elegance of luxury travel.

- **Cards, Panels, and Flight Cards**: 4px radius (`0.25rem`) provides clean, tailored structure reminiscent of premium paper tickets and architectural aircraft panels.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields**: 4px radius (`0.25rem`), providing a tactile, stable anchor.
- **Aircraft Seat Icons**: Rounded 2px to 4px to emulate tailored leather seating contours without drifting into generic cartoon geometry.
- **Status Chips & Cabin Class Badges**: Fully preserved soft rounding (4px) with uppercase tracking.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Select Flight", "Confirm Seats")**: Crimson background (`#D71921`), white label, 48px height, subtle hover deepening to `#B3141B`.
- **Luxury Secondary ("Upgrade to First Class")**: Warm porcelain background, gold border (`#C5A059`), dark navy text, transitioning on hover to a soft gold wash (`#F7F2E7`).
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Ghost styling with dark navy typography and underline micro-animations on hover.

### Flight Search Result Cards
- Container: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px `#E5E1D8` border.
- Layout: Horizontal timeline displaying departure time, duration, stops (with gold dot badge for DXB hub layovers), and arrival time.
- Fare Matrix: Segmented cards displaying tier columns (Special, Saver, Flex, Flex Plus). Selected fare reveals a Crimson top border accent (3px).

### Cabin Seat Selection Map (A380 / Boeing 777)
- **Deck Switcher**: Upper Deck vs. Main Deck toggle buttons for A380 fleets.
- **Fuselage Structure**: Clean architectural walls with exit row indicators, galley icons, and restroom indicators rendered in `#565D6D`.
- **Seat States**:
  - *Available*: Crisp 1px border matched to cabin class color with white interior.
  - *Occupied*: Solid `#D2D6DC` fill with an understated diagonal micro-slash.
  - *Selected*: Solid Crimson (`#D71921`) with high-contrast white seat number and passenger avatar badge.
  - *Bassinet / Extra Legroom*: Distinct gold corner glyph signaling unique seat attributes.

### Flight Booking Summary Sidebar
- Sticky panel adhering to Level 2 elevation.
- Houses real-time itinerary milestones, cabin baggage allowances, skywards miles earned, and tax-inclusive breakdown.
- Primary CTA remains pinned at the base of the panel on desktop and docks to a bottom sheet bar on mobile devices.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- Border: 1px `#E5E1D8` on `#FFFFFF` background.
- Focus: Crisp 1.5px border in Slate Navy (`#1B2232`) with zero intrusive glow rings.
- Floating label structure displaying destination city, airport code, and passenger count.
